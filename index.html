Stays in front, even on buggy boxes

Plenty of car boxes let you choose a different launcher and then quietly switch back to their own a while later: after a restart, after a phone connection ends, or for no reason at all.

Road Trip's Keep in front watches for that and brings you back in a split second. It only sees which app is on top, never what's on your screen, and it never interrupts apps you open yourself. Need the box's own launcher? Long-press the clock and open it for two minutes.

Install it

Road Trip isn't in an app store, so you install the APK yourself. It takes a couple of minutes.

  1. Download the APK on the car box, or copy it over on a USB stick. Latest version.
  2. Open it from the file manager. If Android asks, allow installing apps from that source.
  3. Make it your home app. Press Home and choose Road Trip → Always, or tap Set as home app in the banner on Road Trip's home screen.
  4. Pick your favorite apps when Road Trip asks: three to five of the ones you use most.
  5. Turn on Keep in front. Tap Turn on in the banner, then switch on Road Trip: keep in front in Accessibility. On Android 13 or newer the switch may be greyed out as a "restricted setting": tap it once, then go to Settings → Apps → Road Trip → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, and switch it on. Road Trip walks you through this.
